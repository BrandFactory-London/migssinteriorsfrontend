Kitchen questions

The things people ask before they book

What does a bespoke kitchen cost?

Most of our kitchens run from £35,000 to £90,000 all in — cabinetry, stone, appliances, building work and decoration. Structural openings and appliance choices move the number most. The quote is fixed in writing before we start.

How do we cook while the kitchen is out?

We set up a temporary kitchen — sink, fridge, worktop and hob — in another room before strip-out, and leave it until your new one is signed off. It is included, not an extra.

Can you remove a wall to open the space up?

Yes — we handle the engineer's calculations, the steels, the building control notification and the sign-off certificate as part of the same project. No separate builder to coordinate.

Do you fit kitchens we have bought elsewhere?

We do, and we will review the plan first — showroom layouts sometimes ignore what is behind the wall. Our labour guarantee then covers our installation and building work rather than the units themselves.
